# Click Before Frames

An input assist for Geometry Dash 2.2081. If you click a little too early, the mod predicts what would happen and holds your click until it's safe. If your click was fine, it's never touched.

A from-scratch recreation of the Click Before Frames idea by vVexis.

Use it for practice and fun, not for leaderboard or submitted runs.

## How to use

1. Click the blue **V** button. It shows on every screen.
2. Pick a **mode**. **LOW**, **MED** and **HIGH** set how many ticks a click may be held back. **OFF** turns the whole mod off.
3. Under **Active in**, choose which gamemodes the mod works in.
4. **Drag** the V button anywhere. Its size is in the mod settings.
5. In a level, the pause menu has a **V** button that opens the settings without leaving the level.

## What it does

- **Cube, Ball, Robot, Spider:** if you click in the air a little before landing or reaching an orb, a hidden copy of your icon simulates the click. If your click would kill you but a slightly later one wouldn't, it holds the click until then. Clicks on the ground or on an orb are never delayed.
- **Ship, UFO, Swing, Wave:** very short taps are stretched to a minimum length, so quick taps come out consistent.
- **Not supported:** dual mode, two-player levels and platformer.

## Ticks

A tick is 1/240 of a second. 4 ticks is about 1 frame at 60 FPS.

## Stats

The V menu shows live numbers: `clicks`, `air`, `safe`, `would-die`, `delayed` and `stretched`.
